CAPTAIN GATOR'S HALLOWEEN QUEST — PLAYABLE PROTOTYPE

Upload index.html to the root of your GitHub Pages repository.
Open https://jasonhobbs-bot.github.io/gilbert-halloween/

Controls:
Keyboard: Left/Right or A/D, Jump Space/Up, Knock E.
Touch: on-screen left/right, Jump, Treat.

Six worlds with three levels each. Clear all three levels to unlock the next world.
Candy and mystery clues save locally on the device.
Teacher interactions are ROLE-BASED DEMO CONTENT. They do not use actual Gilbert staff names or photos yet.

This is a first playable prototype with original Canvas vector artwork. It is not a finished professional sprite-art game.
